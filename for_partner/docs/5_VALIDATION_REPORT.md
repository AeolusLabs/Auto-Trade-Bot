# Validation report - SME Baseline v1 (Krypt review, 2026-10-03)

Reproduce: `python code/validate.py` (about 25 s, no MT5). The detailed tables are below the summary.

## Verdict: promising, not proven. Demo forward test only. Do not go live.

**Reproduced exactly.** `python code/backtest.py` gives 180 trades +52.4R (M3) and 437 trades +117.1R (H1) on this machine.
(The H1 data hash printed "DIFFERENT DATA FILE": git converted line endings; the numbers are identical.)

**What holds up**
- **Costs.** Adding 0.40 price units per trade (about 3x the modelled spread) still leaves H1 at +95R, PF 1.39. Break-even extra cost is about 2.2 (H1) and 1.05 (M3) price units per trade. The edge is not a spread artefact.
- **No parameter cliff.** All 9 neighbouring settings (stop buffer, order expiry, minimum zone) stay positive on both datasets (H1 +96R to +125R, M3 +28R to +52R).
- **Bias is not decoration (H1).** Inverting the session bias turns H1 negative (-21.6R). Shuffling the session bias gives a median +59R against +117R real (permutation p = 0.025). The blocks carry most of the edge on their own (no bias: +113.8R, PF 1.39) and the bias adds quality (PF 1.39 -> 1.51).
- **Bootstrap.** Chance the 3-year total is <= 0 is 0.6%; the one-month M3 total is 4.3%.

**What does not**
1. **Not significant after the 64-variant search.** t-stat of mean R is 2.33 (H1) and 1.65 (M3); the best t expected from 64 no-edge variants is about 2.4. Neither clears it. (Variants overlap, so the bar is conservative, but this is "suggestive", not "established".)
2. **M3 bias is not significant** (permutation p = 0.17) and inverted bias is still +12R on M3: one month cannot separate the bias from luck.
3. **The edge is concentrated.** Two quarters (2025-Q3 +41R, 2023-Q4 +24R) supply 65R of the 117R. 9 of 13 quarters are positive. **The last three H1 quarters are +18.9R, -0.6R, -0.2R** and the second half of September on M3 is +3.6R (PF 1.06). Recent behaviour looks flat.
4. **Drawdown will probably exceed the backtest's.** The backtest max drawdown is 11.7R (H1) / 13.1R (M3), but resampling the same trades gives a *median* 20.9R / 15.2R and a 95th percentile of 38R / 28R. At 0.5% risk, 25R = 12.5% of the account. Expect to meet Allan's "stop at 25R" rule in normal variance, so it is a pause-and-review point, not proof of a broken system. Losing streaks of 18-22 are within the 95-99th percentile.
5. **Fill optimism is the largest unknown.** Limit orders fill on first touch with no queue and no slippage; the cost stress above brackets slippage but cannot replace live fills.

**Decision for both owners:** run the demo forward test (stage 3). The pass criteria in `docs/6_RISKGATE_AND_CHANGES.md` section 4 are a proposal to be agreed and fixed before the first trade.
