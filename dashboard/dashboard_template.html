Validation summary

docs/5_VALIDATION_REPORT.md plus the tail check below

Holds up

An extra 0.40 cost per trade still leaves +95R. All 9 neighbouring settings stay positive. Inverting the bias loses 21.6R. Chance the 3-year total is zero or negative: 0.6%.

Does not

The t-stat is 2.33 (H1) and 1.65 (M3) against about 2.4 expected from the best of 64 no-edge variants. Bias on M3 is not significant (p = 0.17).

Also found

Five trades supply 65% of the total R and the total is about zero without the best ten. Long trades earned +110.5R and short trades +6.5R, in a period when gold roughly doubled.
